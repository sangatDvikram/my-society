# Society Management App — Feature Menu (Plain-Language Guide)

**Who this is for:** Society committee members / managers — no tech background needed.

**What this is:** A simple menu of everything the app *can* do. You don't need all of it on day one. Tick ✅ what your society wants, leave the rest unchecked, and send this back to us. We'll use your selections to work out the cost and build a plan around exactly what you need — nothing more, nothing less.

**How to use it:** Each feature below has a checkbox like this: `- [ ]`. Change it to `- [x]` (or just mark it on paper / tell us verbally) for anything you want included. Features are grouped into topics so you can say yes to a whole topic or just pick a few items inside it.

---

## Always Included (not optional — these are the foundation)

Every society on this platform gets these automatically, regardless of what you pick below:

- A **mobile app** and a **website** for residents, for the committee, and for us (the platform operator).
- Each society's data is kept **completely separate and private** from every other society using the platform.
- Sensitive information — phone numbers, bank details, ID proof — is **encrypted**, not stored as plain readable text.
- Nothing is ever secretly, permanently deleted. Records are safely archived in a way that respects both Indian law (the DPDP Act) and international privacy law (GDPR), and residents can request their data be erased.
- Any money-related action is **double-checked on our secure servers** — we never simply trust what a phone app says happened.
- A support/admin team can manage records (societies, flats, residents) with every action logged, so there's always a trail of who did what.

## 1. Visitor & Gate Management

*Replaces the paper visitor register at your gate.*

- [ ] Guard verifies walk-in visitors using a one-time code sent to the visitor's phone
- [ ] Guard can take a photo of the visitor (only with consent)
- [ ] Residents get an instant phone notification to **approve or reject** a visitor before they're let in, with a short timeout if they don't respond
- [ ] Residents can **pre-approve** expected guests (delivery, cab, friends) using a QR code or OTP, so the guard lets them straight in without calling anyone
- [ ] Guards can register **recurring passes** for domestic staff (maid, cook, driver) so they don't need re-verification every single day
- [ ] Automatic alert if a visitor stays unusually long (e.g. beyond 4 hours)
- [ ] Vehicle number logging for visitor vehicles

## 2. Maintenance & Repair Requests

*Replaces the maintenance complaint register / WhatsApp group chaos.*

- [ ] Residents can raise a repair request (plumbing, electrical, etc.) from their phone
- [ ] Residents can track the status of their request until it's resolved
- [ ] Full history of past requests, searchable by date and category
- [ ] Admin can assign requests to specific staff or vendors

## 3. Online Payments & Maintenance Dues

*Lets residents pay society dues online instead of cash/cheque, and gives the committee a clear picture of who's paid.*

- [ ] Residents can pay maintenance bills online via UPI, debit/credit card
- [ ] **Auto-pay** — residents set up a standing instruction so dues are paid automatically every month without anyone having to remember
- [ ] Automatic PDF receipts/invoices for every payment, downloadable anytime
- [ ] Society's bank account is verified before any money starts flowing, so payments never go to the wrong place
- [ ] Clear tracking of pending, successful, failed, and refunded payments
- [ ] Large refunds require sign-off from two admins, as an extra safety check

## 4. Facility Booking

*For clubhouses, gyms, swimming pools, party halls, gardens — anything bookable.*

- [ ] Residents can browse available facilities with photos, descriptions, and pricing
- [ ] Residents can request to book a facility for a private event on a specific date/time
- [ ] Committee approves or rejects booking requests
- [ ] Committee can block out dates (e.g. for maintenance or a society-wide event) so nobody can book then
- [ ] Flexible pricing — flat fee, hourly rate, or rates that vary by day/time (e.g. weekends cost more)

## 5. Community Events

*For festivals, tournaments, movie nights, and other society get-togethers.*

- [ ] Residents or committee can create and publish an event for everyone to see
- [ ] Residents can RSVP, with an automatic cap once the event is full
- [ ] Photo and video sharing for each event (a mini gallery per event)
- [ ] Committee can remove inappropriate photos/videos or cancel an event
- [ ] Events can be linked to a facility booking (e.g. "Clubhouse booked for this event")

## 6. Tenant & Rental Management

*For owners who rent out their flat and need to keep the society informed.*

- [ ] Owners can mark their flat as rented and add their tenant's details
- [ ] Owners can securely upload the rent agreement and tenant's ID proof
- [ ] Automatic reminders before the rent agreement expires, so nobody forgets to renew
- [ ] When a tenant moves out, the flat automatically goes back to "vacant" and old records are safely archived
- [ ] Old tenant data is cleaned up automatically after a safety period, in line with privacy law

## 7. Vendor Directory

*A trusted list of plumbers, electricians, pest control, etc. that the committee has vetted.*

- [ ] Committee maintains a directory of approved vendors with contact details
- [ ] Vendor phone numbers are protected — every time a resident reveals a vendor's number, it's logged for safety/accountability
- [ ] Vendor documents (licence, insurance, ID) stored securely

## 8. Annual Financial Audit Reports

*For the yearly society accounts that every committee has to produce.*

- [ ] Automatic generation of a full yearly financial report (all money received and spent, by category and month)
- [ ] Report can be scheduled to auto-generate at year-end, or triggered manually by the admin
- [ ] Once published, the report is tamper-proof — it cannot be secretly edited later
- [ ] Residents can download their own personal payment statement for tax filing purposes
- [ ] Reports are kept on file for 7 years, as required by law

## 9. Photos & Videos Across the App

*The behind-the-scenes engine that makes photos/videos load fast everywhere — facility photos, event galleries, vendor logos, etc.*

- [ ] Fast-loading, automatically-resized photos and videos on both mobile and web, so pages don't feel slow
- [ ] All photos/videos stored privately — never publicly accessible without permission

## 10. Notifications & Announcements

- [ ] Push notifications on residents' phones for approvals, reminders, and important updates
- [ ] A society-wide announcement board for the committee to post notices

## 11. Multi-Language Support

- [ ] App available in **English**
- [ ] App available in **Hindi**
- [ ] App available in **Marathi**
